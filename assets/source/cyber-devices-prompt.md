# Cyber device sprites

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, 2026-09-08. Source: `cyber-devices-sheet.png`, 1536 × 1024 RGBA. Cut into six equal 512 px cells, then resized to 256 px with Sharp, preserving alpha. Rows: water / feeder. Columns: levels 1 / 2 / 3. No palette or geometry replacement in code. Reservoir contents are live overlays inside the generated windows.

Use case: stylized-concept. Production sprite sheet for a CYBERPUNK virtual pet game. Exactly 3 columns x 2 rows SIX standalone equipment sprites. Truly transparent alpha background: NO background, NO floor, NO glow haze, NO shadow, no backdrop color or checkerboard. Each equal square cell has 18 percent empty padding. Top row water dispensers levels 1,2,3; bottom row automatic pet food dispensers levels 1,2,3. Left simple compact tech, middle upgraded armored modular tech, right premium sophisticated angular tech; visibly growing complexity, not just recolors. Matte charcoal gunmetal housings, beveled hard surfaces, restrained cyan circuit LED lines for water, magenta/amber lines for feeders, small status lights, premium futuristic industrial design. Three-quarter isometric view, sharp readable game inventory silhouettes. IMPORTANT: every device has a large FRONT-FACING rectangular OPEN RESERVOIR WINDOW, EMPTY and dark with no contents, consistent simple rectangular perspective opening in front center, ready for dynamic contents to be rendered by game engine. No water, no food inside. Separate external small pet-access bowl below window on every device. No written text, no numerals, no logos, no animal face decorations, no gold cute toys, no rounded pastel household appliances. All six entire objects contained and separated. 3:2 canvas.
